# diversityframework
## Making Room in FOSS

A handbook for community builders. A step-by-step guide to finding who your community reaches, who it misses, and what to do about it.

**Status:** working draft. The five areas and scoring scale are proposed organising tools, not a validated assessment or certification of inclusion.
